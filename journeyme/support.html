Using JourneyMe

Swipe to explore nearby pages, use the coordinate teleporter to visit a specific location, and tap an open area to contribute. Open a contributor’s profile by tapping their contribution.

Report or block

Press and hold a contribution to report it. To block someone, open their profile and choose Block User. You can review blocked accounts in Settings.

Delete your account

In JourneyMe, open Settings, tap Delete Account, and confirm. This permanently deletes your account, profile, and contributions.

Suspended accounts

If you believe your account was suspended by mistake, email us with the subject “JourneyMe Suspension Appeal.” Do not include your password.

Contact

Email journeymeproject@gmail.com. Please include a short description of the issue and the app version when possible. Never send your password.
